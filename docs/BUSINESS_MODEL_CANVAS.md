# ModelFit · Draft Business Model Canvas

**All commercial statements below are hypotheses to test. No customers, partnerships, funding, procurement access or validated savings are claimed.** Rising Stars, UAE Hackathon 2026.

| Canvas area | Working hypothesis | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer segments | Government service owners, AI evaluation teams and accountable approvers need a shared suitability record. Auditors are secondary users. | Interviews across at least three distinct teams; observed workflow and authority maps. |
| Value proposition | Context-specific evidence, justified reuse and change-driven reassessment could reduce repeated work while improving traceability. | Baseline-to-pilot comparison; evidence-quality review; user willingness to adopt. |
| Channels | Challenge demonstrations, invited discovery sessions and future controlled pilots could reach suitable teams. | Permission to approach teams; actual engagement and procurement constraints. |
| Relationships | Assisted onboarding, clear governance ownership and periodic evidence review may support adoption. | Pilot support logs and a named operational owner. |
| Revenue | An annual organizational subscription or workflow-support contract may be viable after validation. No price is assumed. | Willingness-to-pay interviews, procurement/legal review and cost-to-serve estimates. |
| Key activities | Maintain evaluation traceability, validate applicability rules, improve source quality, test change impact and support reviewers. | Repeatable quality and security procedures. |
| Key resources | Product engineering, evaluation expertise, governance expertise, trustworthy source corpora and verified identity. | Skills inventory, hiring/capacity plan and data permissions. |
| Key partners | Potential future identity, hosting, evaluation and domain-advice providers; none are committed. | Explicit agreements, verified responsibilities and access permissions. |
| Cost structure | Engineering and review time, secure hosting/database, identity, evaluation calls, accessibility and security assurance. | Measured pilot usage, actual vendor quotes and personnel effort. |

The competition prototype demonstrates the workflow and differentiating What Changed? engine. Commercial and organizational viability remain open questions. A pilot should not turn prototype approval logic into an organizational policy without accountable validation.
